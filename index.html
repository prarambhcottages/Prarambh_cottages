ABOUT PRARAMBH

Experience Uttarakhand's Untouched Wilderness

Welcome to Prarambh Cottages & Camp, where mountain breezes, whispering pines, and panoramic valley views set the stage for your perfect escape. Located near Nainital at Gethiya Padav, we blend rustic outdoor adventure with modern comfort.
